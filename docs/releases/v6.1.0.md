# v6.1.0 "Anselmo"

> **Superseded on the switch height:** v6.2.0 moves Midstate Walk forward
> to block **371,500**, expected around 10 November 2026. This release still
> switches at 420,000 and rejects the first Walk block: run v6.2.0 or later.

The desktop wallet now holds MDW, midwimble's coin, beside MDS. It runs
midwimble's node, swaps MDS for MDW with nobody in between, and mines MDW.
Windows gets an installer.

Consensus is v6.0.0's: from block **420,000** the proof of work is Midstate
Walk, and every node must run v6.0.0 or later before then (expected around
mid-December 2026). A node already on v6.0.0 or v6.0.1 needs this release
only for the desktop wallet.

## Swap MDS for MDW

- **Atomic swaps:** the Trade tab is a market between MDS and MDW. Post an
  order or take one, and the two wallets negotiate the swap between
  themselves over the chat bus, encrypted after the take. Each locks its
  coins: either both swaps complete, or each side gets its own coins back
  once its deadline passes. Neither chain's rules changed to allow it; the
  [design note](https://github.com/ciphernom/midstate/blob/v6.1.0/docs/design/mds-mdw-swaps.md)
  explains how.
- **The ETH market is hidden** while it is unreliable.
  `MIDSTATE_ETH_MARKET=1` brings it back.

## MDW in the wallet

- **The same recovery phrase:** the MDW account comes from it, and
  midwimble's own wallets restore it from the same words. A wallet made
  before this release adds it by entering its phrase once.
- **Every tab:** Send and Receive switch between MDS and MDW, and an MDW send
  shows its exact fee. History and Coins list MDW too, and the Dashboard
  shows both balances and both chains.
- **Midwimble's full node runs inside the app**, beside midstate's (P2P port
  9433). The Node tab shows both nodes, and midwimble's block explorer is
  served on this machine (`http://127.0.0.1:9435/`), one click from any MDW
  payment.

## Mine MDW

- **On a pool that merge-mines midwimble:** the miner now gives the pool your
  MDW address, so such a pool pays you MDW as well, with no bond. The Mining
  panel has a preset for one (`rpc.cypherpunk.gold:3333`).
- **Solo, with your own bond:** the Bonds tab locks a bond on this wallet's
  mining key, which comes from your phrase, and registers it with midwimble
  for you, showing how far it has got. Once it is ready, mining on your own
  pool merge-mines MDW, and the MDW blocks it finds pay your MDW account.
- **Bonds:** a new bond must hold at least 128 gMDS (more from 30 November,
  as midwimble's difficulty rises), and it mines only while more than 30 days
  of its lock remain. A ready bond on another key, such as a pool's co-bond,
  has its co-bond file one click away.

## Windows

- **Installer:** `midstate-windows-amd64-setup.exe` installs the wallet for
  your user, with no administrator prompt, and adds Start menu and desktop
  shortcuts. Its uninstaller, under Settings › Apps, leaves your wallet and
  chain data where they are. The zip is still there for anyone who would
  rather unpack it.
- **Icon:** the app shows the Midstate logo in Explorer, on shortcuts and on
  the taskbar.
- Both are unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn the first time you run them:
  choose More info, then Run anyway.
