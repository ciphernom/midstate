# v6.1.1 "Anselmo"

Midwimble's nodes now replace runs of over-cap blocks (midwimble
v1.2.2-DIMEBAG). This release keeps midstate's side in step. The pool gives
back what a replaced midwimble block took from its miners, and the desktop
wallet mines, confirms and swaps MDW by the same rules. The pool's dashboard
also copes with a busy pool.

Consensus is v6.0.0's: from block **420,000** the proof of work is Midstate
Walk, and every node must run v6.0.0 or later before then. **Pools that
merge-mine midwimble should upgrade**, along with the midwimble node they
use, to v1.2.2-DIMEBAG.

## Merge-mining pools

- **Scores come back when midwimble replaces a block:** a midwimble block
  takes the scores it pays when a share finds it. If midwimble later
  replaces the block, it paid nobody. The pool now gives those scores back,
  once, for its next block to pay. It watches each block until 10
  full-weight blocks bury it, or more than 100 blocks do, and the dashboard
  marks it settling or orphaned.
- **Jobs follow the node:** when midwimble's node builds past a run of
  over-cap blocks, the pool rebuilds its job at once, even if the
  midwimble tip has not moved. The dashboard says when a job's block would
  replace such a run.

## The pool's dashboard

- **Search and pages** on every long table: the leaderboard, blocks, both
  midwimble tables, a miner's payouts and rigs, and split verification.
  `#height` finds a block by height, and the block tables find the blocks
  that paid an address. A block lists its first five payees, with the rest
  a click away.
- **Midwimble addresses have their own page:** its hashrate, its shares,
  and what the next block pays it, either exactly or as its chance of being
  drawn. It also estimates MDW a day and lists the blocks that paid it.
- **Pin your address**, in your browser only: a link at the top, your rows
  marked, and "my row" to jump to it.
- **Sortable columns.** Each rig's live hashrate and last share, with idle
  rigs flagged. Live hashrates no longer drop to zero after every block.
  The dashboard also shows how often the pool finds a block.
- It is all worked out in the browser: `/pool/stats` adds only the
  midwimble block's reward, target and fee.

## The desktop wallet

- **Mining MDW builds past over-cap runs** as midwimble's own node does,
  and the local pool rebuilds its job when that changes.
  `MIDSTATE_MDW_NO_ORPHAN=1` keeps it on the tip.
- **Swaps wait for full-weight blocks:** an MDW lock counts as confirmed
  once 2 blocks that counted in full bury it, not just any 2. A lock buried
  under over-cap blocks alone can still be replaced.
- **History shows how settled an MDW payment is:** its confirmations at
  full weight, under the entry, with a hover that says what they mean.
