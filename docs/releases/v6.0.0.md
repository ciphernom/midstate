# v6.0.0 "Anselmo"

A hard fork. From block **420,000**, Midstate's proof of work is
**Midstate Walk**. The codenames come from Pantera's "Walk": this release is
Anselmo, and midwimble's matching release is Dimebag.

**Every node must upgrade before block 420,000**, which is expected around
mid-December 2026 at 60-second blocks. A node still on v5 rejects the first
Walk block and stays on a chain the rest of the network has left.

## Midstate Walk

Each attempt walks 2^18 dependent steps over a 256 KiB scratchpad seeded from
the block header and nonce with BLAKE3.

- Every step reads four scratchpad words at addresses taken from the previous
  step's result.
- It then runs one of eight operations: 64-bit multiply, high multiply,
  divide, an AES round, rotate, add, subtract or xor. The walk redraws their
  order every 4,096 steps.
- No step can start until the one before it has finished.

The design targets CPU cores: the scratchpad fits in a core's L2 cache, and
multiply, divide and AES are fast there. A GPU has to run thousands of walks at
once to stay busy, each with its own 256 KiB. By our model a high-end GPU
comes out roughly level with a 16-core desktop, at 2–3× the power. That figure
is a model; a GPU port has not been benchmarked yet.

- **Cost:** one attempt takes about 3–4 ms on a current x86 core with AES-NI,
  about 20× cheaper than a BLAKE3-chain attempt. `midstate bench-pow` measures
  your machine.
- **Verification:** a Walk block is checked with a single attempt, so headers
  verify faster than BLAKE3-chain ones.
- **Raspberry Pi 5 is the minimum for mining**, because its Cortex-A76 has AES
  instructions. The Pi 3 and Pi 4 builds still run full nodes; their chips
  lack AES instructions, so they compute AES with tables.
- **No new cryptographic assumptions:** BLAKE3 seeds and finishes the walk,
  and the AES round is used only as a mixing function.
- **Sync:** blocks below 420,000 keep the BLAKE3 chain, so syncing from genesis
  works as before.
- **Difficulty:** at the switch the target eases 8× (`WALK_TARGET_SHIFT = 3`),
  and ASERT takes over from there.

[`docs/MIDSTATE_WALK.md`](https://github.com/ciphernom/midstate/blob/main/docs/MIDSTATE_WALK.md)
has the specification, the measurements and golden vectors.
[`docs/walk-reference/`](https://github.com/ciphernom/midstate/tree/main/docs/walk-reference)
has two independent implementations: one in Python, and one in C using ARMv8
AES instructions.

## Miners and pools

- **Algorithm by height:** `midstate miner` and pools pick the algorithm from
  the height. Stratum `mining.notify` carries the job's height as a fifth
  parameter, and a pool that sends four is treated as a BLAKE3-chain pool.
- **Templates:** block templates (HTTP and WebRTC) and pool templates carry
  `height` and `pow` (`"blake3-chain"` or `"walk"`).
- **GPU backend:** `--backend gpu` mines the Walk on the CPU, because the GPU
  kernel is the BLAKE3 chain's. It warns once.
- **Share difficulty:** at the same `--share-bits`, Walk shares arrive about
  20× as often. Pool operators should consider adding 4 at the switch.
- **Midwimble fee:** `midstate pool --midwimble-fee` sets the percentage of
  midwimble rewards the pool keeps (the default is `--fee`).
- **Merged mining:** this continues while both chains take the same proof of
  work. Midwimble switches at its block 86,400, a few hours before midstate
  does. In between, the pool mines midstate alone and its dashboard says why.
- **Third-party miners:** MidstateAxe firmware, GPU miners and scripts must
  switch to the Walk at 420,000. The template's `pow` field says when.

## Browser wallet and SDK

- **Web wallet:** mines whichever proof of work a template asks for.
- **`midstate-sdk` 2.10.0:** does the same, solo and pooled. A custom
  `searchFn` gets `pow` as a fifth argument. This version also fixes worker
  loops that outlived their template and slowed multi-threaded mining after
  every block. Details are in `sdk/CHANGES.md`.

## Also

- Header sync requires consecutive heights, and checks each block's proof of
  work with the algorithm for its height.
