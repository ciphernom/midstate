# v6.3.0 "Anselmo"

**Upgrade the desktop wallet before midwimble's block 37,300**, a few hours
before midstate's 371,500 (expected around 10 November 2026). The v6.2.0
desktop wallet runs a midwimble node from before v1.3-DIMEBAG. That node
takes the Walk at midwimble block 86,400 instead of 37,300, and expects
midstate's at 420,000 instead of 371,500. From 37,300 it leaves the
midwimble network, and its MDW account, trades and bonds stop following the
chain until the wallet is upgraded.

Nodes, pools and `midstate miner` on v6.2.0 need nothing for the switch:
midstate's own rules are unchanged. A pool that merge-mines still needs
midwimble v1.3-DIMEBAG for its midwimble node, as before.

## What changes

- **Desktop: midwimble v1.3-DIMEBAG inside.** The Walk at 37,300, and
  midstate headers checked as Walk proofs from 371,500.
- **Desktop: a simpler Bonds tab.** A new bond is three choices: who mines
  with it (this wallet, or a pool's key for a co-bond), its size, and how
  long it mines. Then Lock and Confirm. The sizes run from what midwimble
  asks now up to the most its price can ask. The wallet registers the bond
  with midwimble once the lock confirms. Each bond is a card showing
  Lock → Register → Mine → Unbond and the one thing to do next, such as
  saving a co-bond file for a pool or unbonding. A test bond replaces
  "Rehearse the exit".
- **Desktop: bond rentals.** Rent a bond out at your price, or rent one to
  mine MDW without locking coins of your own. The coin stays its owner's
  throughout, and a covenant enforces the rent.
- **Desktop: Plans.** Inheritance plans pay each heir its share if the owner
  stops checking in. Payment streams pay a recipient on a schedule,
  cancellable or not. Both run on script from block 371,500, between
  wallets, with nobody holding anyone's coins
  (`docs/design/inheritance-and-streams.md`).
- **Mining: NVIDIA GPUs** mine Midstate Walk through CUDA, beside the CPU or
  instead of it (`--backend auto|gpu|cpu`).
- **`midstate miner`** serves the Axe dashboard itself, at
  `http://127.0.0.1:8546/axe`.
- **Web wallet:** midwimble's mainnet bond rules (128 gMDS, with 30 days of
  lock left).
- **IDE and SDK:** the IDE compiles and runs with the SDK's compiler and VM,
  and the VM enforces the node's stack limits.
- **Removed:** the SDK's token launcher, pump curve and launchpad (SDK
  3.0.0), the website's launchpad page, and the web wallet's site keys.
