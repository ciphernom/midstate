Every share also earns midwimble score for the address your miner gives with --midwimble-address; shares without one count for the pool. A midwimble block pays at most 31 addresses: anyone with 1/31 of the score is paid exactly, the rest by a draw that gives each its share on average. Midwimble amounts are confidential, so blocks show each address's share. A block midwimble replaces pays nobody, so the scores it took go back to the table for the next block.

How to Connect

Provably-fair SPV pool. Your miner mathematically audits the pool every block.

./midstate miner --pool-url stratum+tcp://rpc.cypherpunk.gold:3333 --payout-address <YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS> --midwimble-address <YOUR_MW1_ADDRESS>
